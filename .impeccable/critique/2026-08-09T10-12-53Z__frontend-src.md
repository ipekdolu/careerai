## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Category-interchangeable. With a logo swap, this could serve as a tax calculator or loan approver. The teal and DM Serif Display establish surface branding, but the underlying system is the same upload+textarea+CTA+output pattern across all four features. No job market metaphors, no career-specific visualizations, no vocabulary specific to someone navigating a real job search. The Resume Rewriter's diff approval flow is the one genuinely product-specific pattern. Everything else could belong to any SaaS tool.

## Overall Impression

CareerAI has solid architectural thinking -- the Analyzer to Rewriter to Prep to Interview sequence is a real product insight -- and the visual language is clean and consistent. But the interface is unfinished at the experience layer. Scores are uncontextualized. Errors use browser alerts. Help is completely absent. The single biggest opportunity is calibration: make every number and piece of feedback interpretable so users leave knowing exactly where to focus.

## What's Working

1. **Resume Rewriter's diff approval flow.** Line-by-line approve/reject is the only genuinely product-specific UX pattern in the system. It respects user autonomy over AI output and gives users a sense of control over their own career document.

2. **Semantic color system.** Green/amber/red used consistently across keywords, scores, and diff states maps precisely to the job seeker's mental model. Experienced enough to feel trustworthy without over-explaining.

3. **Feature tab navigation.** Four-card hero grid with selected-state Catalyst border and glow is clear and scannable. Users immediately understand this is a multi-tool workflow.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Silent failures and browser alert() error handling**
- Why it matters: First-time users hitting alert() on a missing field -- or a generic API error -- lose trust immediately. Most common abandonment trigger in form-heavy tools.
- Fix: Replace all alert() calls with inline validation and toast notifications. Add blur-time validation on JD textarea with character count hint. Never wipe the form on error.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Scores and feedback are uncontextualized -- numbers without meaning**
- Why it matters: "72/100" is the hero output but has no anchor. Users don't know if 72 is competitive or disqualifying. Interview score of 5/10 lands as a grade, not a coaching signal.
- Fix: Add benchmark line beneath every score. Frame interview feedback as "What landed well:" before "To strengthen:". Show trajectory, not judgment.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] Analyzer results dump -- 6+ cards with no priority ordering**
- Why it matters: Results present fit score, matched keywords, missing keywords, strengths, gaps, and suggested bullets in flat succession with equal visual weight. No signal about what to act on first.
- Fix: Reorder to (1) Score + one-line verdict + top 3 action items as a summary card. (2) Collapse matched keywords by default -- validating but not actionable. (3) Open missing keywords and suggested bullets by default.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Mock Interview lacks emotional scaffolding and recovery**
- Why it matters: Highest-stakes emotional moment in the product. Getting scored 3/10 with a list of negatives and no positives is demoralizing. Connection drop on Q4 of 6 loses all data.
- Fix: Reorder feedback -- what worked first, then improvement. Add mid-interview status indicator. Persist session to localStorage with resume option on reload.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Context is lost at every feature handoff**
- Why it matters: After Analyzer, navigating to Rewriter requires re-uploading the same resume and re-pasting the same JD. The product presents as an end-to-end workflow but behaves like four independent tools.
- Fix: Persist most recent resume and JD in sessionStorage. Pre-fill inputs on feature switch with a dismissable "Using your recent resume and JD" banner.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

## Minor Observations

- Drag-and-drop not implemented: upload zones look droppable but only register onClick -- no onDrop handler.
- Detector side-tab (Analyzer.jsx:167): borderLeft: 3px solid on suggested bullets is an AI-slop tell. Use full card style to match the rest of the results section.
- Detector layout-transition (MockInterview.jsx:276): Animate transform: scaleX() from a fixed-width container instead of width.
- No loading skeletons: content appears as a visual jump when results load.
- Chat messages are 78% wide on desktop -- unused real estate creates a cramped feeling despite the spacious layout.
- Dark mode contrast unverified: teal on dark card backgrounds (#1a1d27) may fall below WCAG AA 4.5:1.
- Resume parsing not confirmed to user: user could upload a scanned (non-text) PDF and proceed unknowingly to a bad analysis.
