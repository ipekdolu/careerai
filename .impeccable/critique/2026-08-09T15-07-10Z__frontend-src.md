## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Meaningfully improved. The priority actions card, running average score, and "What landed well / To strengthen" language all feel authored for job prep — not generic SaaS. The Analyzer result order (priority → missing keywords → suggested bullets → matched keywords) now tells a story: here's the gap, here's the fix, here's your foundation. The cross-feature handoff (shared resume + JD context) makes the tool feel like a workflow rather than four isolated forms.

What remains category-interchangeable: the Hero copy ("From application to offer — everything you need") is weak differentiation. ErrorBanners are personality-free. Help is completely absent, which makes the most consequential moments (score reveal, interview debrief) feel like output dumps rather than coaching.

**Deterministic scan (0 findings, exit 0):** All 10 source files scanned. No anti-patterns detected. The two issues flagged in the previous run (side-tab borderLeft, layout-transition on width) are confirmed resolved.

## Overall Impression

The polish run moved the score from 20 to 26 — a real +6 gain, mostly on heuristics 3, 4, 7, and 8. The fixes were targeted and effective. H9 (error recovery) improved from 1 to 2 but is still the second-weakest link: ErrorBanner dismissal leaves users stranded with no retry path. H10 (help) remains at 1 — the product's biggest liability. The emotional journey for job seekers who score below 60 is still risky: priority actions help, but the path from "here's your score" to "here's how long it takes to fix" isn't answered.

## What's Working

**1. Priority actions card (new).** Pre-computing the top 3 actionable items from missing keywords + experience gaps is the highest-ROI change in this pass. It answers "what do I do first?" at the exact moment users need it. Specific, numbered, and teal-accented — it reads as a roadmap, not a blame list.

**2. Session restoration in MockInterview.** The banner ("You have an interview in progress. Continue where you left off? Resume / Start fresh") is one of the most user-respecting moments in the product. It acknowledges that real job prep doesn't happen in one uninterrupted sitting. The dual CTA covers both intents cleanly.

**3. Unified drag-drop and input focus states (Analyzer, MockInterview).** Every upload zone now works with drag-drop and shows the Catalyst glow on focus. The interaction model is consistent where it was implemented. These are the kind of details that make a tool feel considered.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] ErrorBanner dismissal is a dead end**
- **Why it matters:** User gets "Analysis failed. Check your connection and try again." — dismisses the banner — and then has no UI signal. The form is intact but there's no "Retry" prompt, no retry button, nothing to indicate the user should try again. Most users will reload the page instead, losing their JD paste.
- **Fix:** Add an optional `onRetry` prop to `ErrorBanner`. Pass a retry callback from each component's submit handler. Banner layout: icon · message · [Retry] [×]. Three components (Analyzer, MockInterview, Prep) have a clear retry action.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Textarea focus styles missing in Prep and ResumeRewriter**
- **Why it matters:** Analyzer and MockInterview have `onFocus/onBlur` handlers that apply the teal border + glow. Prep.jsx and ResumeRewriter.jsx do not. Keyboard and screen-reader users lose the interactive signal on those two inputs — a direct H4 inconsistency and an H3/accessibility gap.
- **Fix:** Add `onFocus` and `onBlur` style handlers to both textarea elements in Prep.jsx and ResumeRewriter.jsx, matching the Analyzer pattern.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] No help, no onboarding — H10 at 1/4**
- **Why it matters:** Users don't know what score to aim for (is 65 close to 70 or far?), why 80 characters matters, how interview scoring works, or what "Culture & fit" questions look like. Every consequential moment produces output without context. First-timers are the primary user — this is a direct product-market fit problem.
- **Fix:** Three lightweight additions: (1) Hoverable `?` tooltips on the score card label ("Scores above 70 are competitive for most roles"), the JD length warning ("Longer descriptions help us match keywords more precisely"), and the interview focus selector. (2) A one-sentence explainer per feature card in Hero ("Practice with AI — get a score and coaching on every answer"). (3) Score tier labels on the Analyzer benchmark text ("Strong match / Good fit / Moderate fit / Significant gaps").
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] MockInterview "..." loading state is ambiguous**
- **Why it matters:** When the AI is generating a response, the chat bubble shows "..." — the same as a human typing indicator in iMessage. Users can't tell if the AI is thinking, the network is slow, or something failed. After 4+ seconds it becomes anxiety-inducing.
- **Fix:** Replace the literal `'...'` text with animated typing dots (CSS animation) and a `"Thinking..."` label below the bubble. If the request exceeds 8 seconds, show an inline "Still working — this can take a moment" message inside the bubble.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate`

**[P2] ResumeRewriter draft approvals not persisted**
- **Why it matters:** MockInterview sessions are saved to `sessionStorage`. The Rewriter's `approved` state and `diff` are not. A user who spends 10 minutes reviewing 20 suggested changes and closes the tab loses everything. The inconsistency is jarring given MockInterview's handling.
- **Fix:** Mirror the `SESSION_KEY` pattern from MockInterview — write `{ diff, approved }` to `sessionStorage` on every toggle, restore on mount with a dismissable banner.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)** — most exposure to remaining gaps
- Gets score of 45. Benchmark text says "Significant gaps. Prioritize the missing keywords and suggested bullets below." — but no guidance on whether this gap is fixable in a week or requires months. Leaves unsure whether to apply.
- In MockInterview, gets 5/10 on Q1. "What landed well" section is encouraging, but "To strengthen" feedback is still clinically phrased ("Be more specific about the outcome"). No example of what a strong answer looks like.
- In Hero, sees "Mock Interview" card but doesn't know what it entails (scored? multi-question? how long?). No explainer. Clicks in blind.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent, screen reader)**
- Hero feature cards are `div` elements with `onClick` — not buttons, no `role="button"`, no `onKeyDown`. Tab navigation reaches them but pressing Enter does nothing. Sam cannot switch features with keyboard alone.
- Upload zones: outer `div` has `onClick` but no `aria-label`. Screen reader announces nothing about the upload affordance.
- All textareas lack `aria-label`. Screen reader reads placeholder text as label — acceptable but not conformant. `<label>` elements are present in the DOM but not linked via `htmlFor`/`id`.
- Color-coded keyword pills (green = matched, red = missing) have no text distinction or `aria-label`. Sam hears "Python" with no pass/fail context.

**Riley (Stress Tester)**
- Uploads 15MB PDF to any upload zone. No client-side file-size check fires (validation is text only). Network request is made; API failure returns a generic error with no size guidance.
- Opens MockInterview in two tabs. Both tabs write to the same `SESSION_KEY`. Whichever tab last triggers a state change overwrites the other's session silently.
- In ResumeRewriter, clicks "Approve All" on 30 bullets. No confirmation. No undo. Immediately downloads. Reviews the PDF and realizes one approved change was wrong — has to restart the full rewrite.

## Minor Observations

- "Needs the most work" in the MockInterview summary modal is harsher than needed; "Next focus area" would end the session on forward momentum rather than criticism.
- `cursor: 'not-allowed'` is set correctly on most disabled buttons but missing on one ResumeRewriter button (generating state, line ~268).
- Prep section has 6 distinct visual card types (QCard, Section divider, tag pills, icon list rows, star list, triangle list). Slightly more visually fragmented than the other features.
- "Rewrite this resume →" CTA at bottom of Analyzer appears only when `onRewrite && resumeFile` — correct guard, but low visual prominence. Could benefit from a stronger call-out after a low score (< 60).

## Questions to Consider

1. **What if the score card showed a one-sentence "time to fix" estimate?** "Your top 3 gaps could be addressed in a resume session this weekend." That closes the gap between "I know what's wrong" and "I know whether it's worth applying now."
2. **Should the Hero explain what each feature produces, not just what it does?** "Resume Analyzer: get a 0–100 fit score with the exact keywords you're missing" is more specific than "Get your fit score." Persuade mode requires a claim.
3. **Is there a natural moment in MockInterview to show an example answer?** After the first question is asked but before the user answers — an optional "Show me an example" expander could calibrate expectations without breaking flow.
